Overview
This project develops a formal, machine-checked development of "Thet systems":
finite-dimensional complex inner-product-space structures combining a partial
isometry θ, a Z₂-grading Γ, and an antiunitary/reality structure J. The work
produces Lean 4 / Mathlib4 code that formalizes definitions and proofs and a
short paper draft explaining the mathematics.

Why sponsor
- Ensures continued development and maintenance of the formalization.
- Funds dedicated time to complete proof gaps, add examples/tests, and improve CI.
- Helps create reliable, reproducible artifacts that other researchers and
  formal-methods engineers can build on.

Planned deliverables (3–9 months)
- Complete Mathlib4-native formalization of J as antilinear/antiunitary maps on vectors and a systematic matrix-lift API.
- Generalized lemmas for partial isometries, interactions with Γ, and consequences of J² = ±1.
- Additional examples (qutrit, block-diagonal models) and unit tests.
- CI configuration: Lake build, LaTeX checks for the paper, and automated tests.
- Documentation and contributor onboarding (CONTRIBUTING.md, examples).
